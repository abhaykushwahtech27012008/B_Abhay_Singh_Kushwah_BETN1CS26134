Quick Questions

Clear answers for new members and competition reviewers.

Who can join the University Coding Club?

Students across CSE and related engineering batches who want to build consistent coding habits, collaborate on projects, and participate in technical events are welcome.

Why is this project built as a two-page static site?

Splitting index.html (overview & dashboard) and about.html (mission, tracks, and architecture) keeps code modular, easy to maintain, and fast to load.

How is responsiveness handled across screen sizes?

CSS Grid, Flexbox, fluid typography, and responsive breakpoints adapt cards, navigation, and buttons across mobile phones, tablets, and desktop monitors.
